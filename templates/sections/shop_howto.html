{# How a grant works, in three sticky notes. Short on purpose: the upgrade idea is the only part that needs saying. #}
  1. Pick a grant.It lands on a Hack Club Bank card, so you buy the real thing yourself.
  2. Want it bigger?Add upgrades to the grant you picked. They top up the same card.
