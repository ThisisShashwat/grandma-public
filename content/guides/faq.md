## Getting started

::: faq Who can join?
Help out grandma is for highschoolers! You need to be 13-18 years old (under 19) to participate.
:::

::: faq Does it cost anything?
100% free\* - all the prizes are donated to us or paid for by us!\
\*customs might occur outside of the US or you might simply get a HCB grant
:::

::: faq Can I pick my own project?
Always. Grandma only decides how you build it, never what.
:::

::: faq What if I get a demand I can't do?
Ask again. The first three asks are free, and after that each ask costs a few cookies.
:::

::: faq Can I use AI?
Use of AI must strictly be under 30% of total time.
:::

::: faq Can I make changes to my old projects and ship it with additional hours?
No, the project must be created AFTER you have asked grandma.
:::

::: faq What kinds of projects count?
All kinds of technical projects, as long as they're open-source.
:::

::: faq Is this legit?
Yup. It's run with [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com/), a nonprofit that has been running programs like this for years. Past rounds include [Arcade](https://hackclub.com/arcade/), [High Seas](https://highseas.hackclub.com/), [Summer of Making](https://summer.hackclub.com/) and [Flavortown](https://flavortown.hackclub.com/). Teens build projects, and real prizes get sent out.
:::

::: faq How do I start?
Join the Slack, set up Hackatime, then talk to Grandma. The [start here](/guides/start-here/) page has all five steps.
:::

::: faq Do I need to know how to code?
You need to want to build something, dear. Beginners are welcome, and time spent learning counts, as long as it is real work on your project. Pick something small and finish it.
:::

::: faq Can I work with a friend?
No. You work alone, and so does everyone else. Grandma has only one lap.
:::

## Rules

::: faq How many projects can I build?
As many as you like, one after another. Only one at a time, and every one starts with a chat with Grandma. See the [rules](/guides/rules/).
:::

::: faq How long do I have for a project?
As long as it takes. Half an hour or ten days, nobody is timing you. The program as a whole will have an end, and the date is not decided yet.
:::

::: faq Can I ship the same project twice?
Software ships once. Hardware is the exception: the design ships first, then the build. [All about hardware](/guides/hardware/).
:::

::: faq Can one project count in two programs?
Not the same work and hours. Time that another program already counted does not count here. Make something new for Grandma.
:::

::: faq Can I submit school work or paid Hack Club work?
No. It has to be your own project, made in your own time.
:::

## Hours and payouts

::: faq How do I earn cookies?
For the hours you built and shipped. Spicier demands and a project Grandma loves make each hour worth more. The [payouts](/guides/payouts/) page has a calculator.
:::

::: faq Will I be paid for every hour I log?
Not word for word, dear. A reviewer may reduce your hours if they look inflated. Grandma can only prove what she can verify, and Hackatime is not proof of work.
:::

::: faq Why were my hours cut?
Because they did not look like the project. The usual reasons are very few commits for a lot of hours, a small project with a big number on it, or a timer that kept running while you were away. When in doubt, the doubt goes against the number.
:::

::: faq Does time spent learning count?
Yes, when it is real work on your project: reading, trying things, getting stuck and unstuck. Idle time does not count.
:::

::: faq When do I get my cookies?
After a reviewer has checked your project and your hours. Then Grandma hands them over herself, the next time you visit. How long the review takes is not decided yet.
:::

## AI

::: faq Is it fine if AI wrote most of it?
No. AI may take under 30% of your total time, and you must be able to explain every part of what you hand in. A project from one prompt, with no debugging, no shaping and no learning, will be turned down.
:::

::: faq Do I have to say how I used AI?
Yes. The hand-in note has a box for it: what for, and roughly how much. Honesty is easier than being caught. How this site was made is on the [AI disclosure](/guides/ai-disclosure/) page.
:::

## Submitting

::: faq What counts as a link where people can try it?
A page anyone can open and use, with no login, that stays awake. A reviewer opens it in a private window and expects to try it in under two minutes. If it needs a login, put demo details in your description.
:::

::: faq Where should I host it?
Somewhere permanent. Not Streamlit, not a free tier that falls asleep, not a tunnel to your own laptop. The hand-in note refuses the hosts Grandma knows about.
:::

::: faq Where do I put a video?
A link to YouTube, Vimeo or a plain mp4 file. Not Google Drive.
:::

::: faq Can my screenshot be a GIF?
No. A still PNG, JPEG or WebP of the real project, not a mock-up.
:::

::: faq Do I need a README, a licence and lots of commits?
A public repository and a README that says what it is and how to run it, yes. A licence is a nice idea, not a must. Commit as you go: one giant commit for a big project makes a reviewer suspicious.
:::

::: faq What if my project is turned down?
Grandma tells you why when you next visit, in her own words. That project is finished, and you can talk to her about a new one.
:::

## Hardware

::: faq What do I hand in for a hardware design?
A parts list with exact names (even the parts you already own), a schematic or wiring diagram if it has electronics, editable CAD files for anything printed, and the firmware if it needs any. An STL on its own is not enough.
:::

::: faq When can I buy a hardware grant?
After your design ship is approved and Grandma has told you so. Then you pick that project and list every part with its link and price. [The details](/guides/hardware/).
:::

::: faq Do I have to hand in the build here?
No. The build ship is up to you: hand it in here, hand it in to another program, or not at all. If you are done with the project, tell Grandma on your project page and you can start another.
:::

## Shop and grants

::: faq What is a grant?
Money on a Hack Club Bank card for one specific thing, which you buy yourself. Grandma does not post parcels, she has a bad hip. [How the Pantry works](/guides/shop-and-grants/).
:::

::: faq What do I have to show to get one?
The link to the product and a screenshot of your whole cart, with tax and shipping in it. Hardware grants also want every part listed with its price.
:::

::: faq What if the thing costs more where I live?
Add upgrade grants until the grant covers it. Upgrades are never sold alone, only together with their base grant.
:::

## Account

::: faq How do I get an account?
Use the Sign up button on the home page. Accounts are made by hand, so if yours is not there yet, ask in <span data-slack="help"></span>. There is no reset button either: if you forget your password, ask there too.
:::

::: faq Who can see my projects?
Everybody. Your display name, your Slack ID, your projects and what you hand in are public. Your region and time zone are not. Put nothing in a project that you would not put on a wall.
:::

::: faq Where do I ask more questions?
In Slack. Ask for help in <span data-slack="help"></span> and chat in <span data-slack="grandma"></span>. Somebody there is less grumpy than Grandma.
:::
