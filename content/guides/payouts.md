You do not get paid in money, dear. You get paid in cookies.

## Cookies

You earn cookies for the hours you built and shipped. The Pantry sells things for cookies, and every price there shows what the grant is worth. [The shop](/guides/shop-and-grants/) explains the rest.

## Grandma's approval meter

Not every hour is worth the same. Each hour is multiplied by how much Grandma approves of your project:

- **More demands and spicier demands** raise it.
- **Grandma loved it:** a really cool, well made project raises it.
- **Huge project:** a project of 20 hours or more raises it.
- **A Blue Ribbon** raises it a little more. [All about the ribbon](/guides/blue-ribbon/).

Pick no demand and Grandma is furious, which is why every project has at least one.

## Try it

Pick some demands, slide the hours, and watch her face. The face is a mood meter and nothing else, the number is what counts.
