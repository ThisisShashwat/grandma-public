The three steps

  1. Step 1: Find x

    x equals the sum of the demand tiers you kept plus the bonuses that apply, but never more than the largest x (xmax).

    x = min(xmax, sum of kept demand tiers + quality + huge + ribbon + ribbon_holder)

    Filled in with Grandma's numbers:

    x = min({{ config.xmax }}, kept demands + bonuses)

    Where mild is {{ config.tier[1] }}, medium is {{ config.tier[2] }}, and spicy is {{ config.tier[3] }}. Bonuses are: Grandma loved it ({{ config.quality }}), huge project at {{ config.huge_hours }} hours or more ({{ config.huge }}), Blue Ribbon ({{ config.ribbon }}), and held a Blue Ribbon before ({{ config.ribbon_holder }}).

  2. Step 2: Calculate the multiplier

    Grandma uses an exponential curve so the multiplier climbs faster as you stack harder challenges:

    multiplier = 1 + (cap - 1) * (1 - e^(-k * x)) / (1 - e^(-k * xmax))

    Filled in with Grandma's numbers:

    multiplier = 1 + ({{ config.cap }} - 1) * (1 - e^(-({{ config.k }}) * x)) / (1 - e^(-({{ config.k }}) * {{ config.xmax }}))

  3. Step 3: Calculate cookies

    Multiply cookies for one plain hour by the multiplier and your approved hours:

    cookies = cookies for a plain hour * multiplier * approved hours

    Filled in with Grandma's numbers:

    cookies = {{ base_cookies }} * multiplier * approved hours

Worked example: one mild demand, 12 hours

  1. Step 1 (find x): One mild demand gives tier value {{ worked.x }}. With 12 hours (under {{ config.huge_hours }} hours), no extra bonuses apply.
    x = min({{ config.xmax }}, {{ worked.x }}) = {{ worked.x }}
  2. Step 2 (calculate multiplier): Substitute x into Grandma's curve formula:
    multiplier = 1 + ({{ config.cap }} - 1) * (1 - e^(-({{ config.k }}) * {{ worked.x }})) / (1 - e^(-({{ config.k }}) * {{ config.xmax }}))
    multiplier = 1 + {{ worked.cap_minus_1 }} * (1 - e^({{ worked.exp_num_pow }})) / (1 - e^({{ worked.exp_den_pow }}))
    multiplier = 1 + {{ worked.cap_minus_1 }} * (1 - {{ worked.exp_num }}) / (1 - {{ worked.exp_den }})
    multiplier = 1 + {{ worked.cap_minus_1 }} * ({{ worked.term_num }}) / ({{ worked.term_den }})
    multiplier = 1 + {{ worked.cap_minus_1 }} * {{ worked.ratio }} = {{ worked.multiplier_4dp }}
  3. Step 3 (calculate cookies): Multiply cookies for one plain hour by the multiplier and your approved hours:
    cookies = {{ worked.base_cookies }} * {{ worked.multiplier_4dp }} * {{ worked.hours }} = {{ worked.raw_cookies_2dp }}
    Rounded half up gives {{ worked.shown_cookies }} cookies.
