How it works

  1. 1Tell her what you're building

    Pick a category: web, software, games, electronics or CAD. The project is always your idea.

  2. 2She phones with a demand

    Grandma pulls a note out of the cookie jar. It says how you have to build, like no libraries or one button. Don't like it? Ask again. The first three asks are free.

  3. 3Build for 10 to 15 days, then ship

    Log your hours with Hackatime and ship the project. You earn cookies for your hours, and more when Grandma is impressed.

Questions Grandma gets asked

Who can join?

Help out grandma is for highschoolers! You need to be 13-18 years old (under 19) to participate.

Does it cost anything?

100% free* - all the prizes are donated to us or paid for by us!
*customs might occur outside of the US or you might simply get a HCB grant

Can I pick my own project?

Always. Grandma only decides how you build it, never what.

What if I get a demand I can't do?

Ask again. The first three asks are free, and after that each ask costs a few cookies.

Can I use AI?

Use of AI must strictly be under 30% of total time.

Can I make changes to my old projects and ship it with additional hours?

No, the project must be created AFTER you have asked grandma.

What kinds of projects count?

All kinds of technical projects, as long as they're open-source.

Is this legit?

Yup. It's run with Hack Club, a nonprofit that has been running programs like this for years. Past rounds include Arcade, High Seas, Summer of Making and Flavortown. Teens build projects, and real prizes get sent out.
