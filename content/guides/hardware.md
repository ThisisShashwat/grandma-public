Software is easy: you build it, you ship it, done. Hardware has to exist in the real world, and Grandma allows it two ships for that.

## Two ships

1. **The design.** Ship your project as a design first.
2. **The build.** After you built it for real, you may ship it again.

It is the same project both times. This is the only kind of project that may ship twice. Everything else ships once: see the [rules](/guides/rules/).

## The design ship

You start the way everyone does: you talk to Grandma first and get your [demands](/guides/demands/). Electronics and CAD have their own demands, like "Battery Only" and "No Supports". Then you design your project around them and ship the design.

Reviewers check it by hand, with our own review screens. No special code, just a person looking.

## The hardware grant

Once your design is shipped, you can pick that project and order a **hardware grant** for it. A hardware grant belongs to that one project and cannot be used for any other. You list every item you are buying, with its link and price, and attach screenshots. The rest works like any other grant: [the shop guide](/guides/shop-and-grants/).

## The build ship

When you have built it, you can ship it again, to this program or to any other program. Grandma does not mind.
