The Blue Ribbon is for the projects that make Grandma stop knitting and put her glasses down.

## What it takes

A project earns one when it is huge and also very, very good. It is rare on purpose. If everyone got one it would just be a ribbon.

## What you get

- **A bigger bonus** on your cookies. It is part of the [payout maths](/guides/payouts/), so you can see it in the calculator.
- **A spot on Grandma's wall.** Your project gets pinned up where everybody can see it.
